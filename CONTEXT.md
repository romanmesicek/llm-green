# LLM Green

A local dashboard that turns the token usage of AI coding agents (Claude Code, Mistral Vibe) into an ecological footprint. The footprint is the headline; usage and limits are supporting information.

## Language

**Footprint**:
The estimated ecological impact of inference: energy (Wh), CO₂ (gCO2e) and water (mL). The primary metric of the app.
_Avoid_: Impact score, emissions (when meaning all three)

**Usage**:
The token counts per provider, model and project, shown as supporting information next to the footprint.
_Avoid_: Consumption, spend

**Limit**:
A subscription rate-limit window of a provider (Claude: 5 hours and 7 days), shown as supporting information.
_Avoid_: Quota (in UI text), budget
